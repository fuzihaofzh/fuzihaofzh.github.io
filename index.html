I am a Research Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I study large language models through the lens of linguistics, cognition, and fairness — how these models represent language, why they behave the way they do, and how to make them fair and trustworthy. I am also an Affiliated Lecturer at the Language Technology Lab (LTL), University of Cambridge, and a Research Associate at the Oxford Internet Institute (OII), University of Oxford.

Before joining CUHK, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the OII working with Prof. Chris Russell, Prof. Brent Mittelstadt, and Prof. Sandra Wachter, and before that a Research Associate at LTL, University of Cambridge, working with Prof. Nigel Collier. I received my Ph.D. from The Chinese University of Hong Kong under the supervision of Prof. Wai Lam, and was a visiting student at the NLP Lab of Tsinghua University with Prof. Zhiyuan Liu. Prior to my Ph.D., I spent two years at Alibaba Cloud developing large-scale distributed machine learning algorithms for the PAI platform.

My work has been published at venues including NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, AAAI, and FAccT, and is supported by the NVIDIA Academic Grant and the AMD University Program AI Award. I also serve as an Area Chair for ACL Rolling Review (ARR).
